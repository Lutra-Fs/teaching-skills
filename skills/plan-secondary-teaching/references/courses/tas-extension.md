# Other TAS courses (extension support)

Food Technology, Industrial Technology, Textiles Technology, Graphics Technology, Agricultural Technology, Engineering Studies and every other NESA TAS course without its own card, 7–10 or Stage 6.

Lock the live syllabus (`../curriculum-lock.md`), inherit local material, and produce full drafts labelled `extension`, with outcome codes and course requirements taken from the locked syllabus. `extension` means aligned to the syllabus text but unchecked by a specialist, so list the checks still owed before classroom or formal-assessment use:

- exact course, cohort, syllabus version and school adoption evidence;
- official outcomes, content, course requirements, assessment and record of changes;
- faculty program, assessment schedule and template, specialist exemplar where available;
- subject-content accuracy (for example nutrition or food-science claims), practical feasibility and assessment validity;
- the school's WHS controls for workshop, kitchen or machinery work.

Do not reuse Technology 7–8 Food and agricultural practices outcomes or requirements for Food Technology. Industrial Technology Timber is a course; "timber" in Technology 7–8 is only a material context.

For Stage 6 courses, resolve course type, course specifications, indicative hours, fixed modules, school-based assessment components and weightings, projects or submitted work, and the HSC examination where one exists.

Workshop and food runtime checks: `tas-common.md`.
