# Design and Technology routes

"D&T" is ambiguous. Force one exact route before planning. Course facts below are an 11 August 2026 routing snapshot: recheck the live course page, implementation advice and record of changes for each new cohort or version (see `../curriculum-lock.md`).

1. **Design and Technology 7–10 (2019):** current during the 2026 snapshot ([course page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/tas/design-and-technology-7-10-2019)). A 100-hour route covers at least 2 context areas through 2–4 units; a 200-hour route at least 3 context areas through 4–8 units. Majority practical experience, an approved brief, the design process, project evidence and local workshop and material controls.
2. **Design Innovation Technology 7–10 (2025):** new from 2028; earlier adoption only by sector or school decision. Never migrate a 2019 program just because the new page exists. Lock its focus areas and requirements from the [current NESA page](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/design-innovation-technology-7-10-2025/overview/course).
3. **Design and Technology Stage 6 (2013):** a separate 2-unit Board Developed course ([course page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/tas/design-and-technology-stage-6-2013)). Lock Year 11 versus Year 12, the Year 11 design-project requirements, school assessment, written examination, Major Design Project, folio, supervision and authorship, progress records and submission rules. The MDP is not a generic classroom project, and AI planning cannot certify submitted work.

## Continuity artefacts

Approved brief and criteria, folio state, investigation and research, concepts and drawings, prototype or product, testing and evaluation evidence, material and tool competence, progress and authorship record, submitted-work milestone.

Workshop runtime checks and common design guidance: `tas-common.md`.
