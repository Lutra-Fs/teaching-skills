# Computing Technology 7–10

Use the [current course requirements](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/computing-technology-7-10-2022/overview/course). Course facts below are an 11 August 2026 routing snapshot: recheck the live course page, implementation advice and record of changes for each new cohort or version (see `../curriculum-lock.md`).

## Requirements to verify

- The 100-hour or 200-hour route; program all outcomes for either.
- 100 hours: at least one Enterprise Information Systems focus area and one Software Development focus area, 2–3 in total.
- 200 hours: at least two focus areas from each branch, 4–6 in total.
- Practical and project work for most of the course time, including at least one group project; keep individual evidence inside it.
- Stage 4 uses `CT4-ADJ-01` and permits adjusting Stage 5 outcomes for Years 7–8. Stage 4 outcomes come only from that adjustment; there is no CT4 subject-outcome family.

## Reading school materials

- The functional requirements (purpose, use cases, test cases) and non-functional requirements dot points are worded the same in every focus area, so school materials often reuse app-development examples and skip test cases and non-functional requirements. Check those two explicitly.
- Classroom may hold paired series under one number (a long "Lesson N" theory lesson and a short "LN" practical). Ask the teacher before treating a repeated number as a conflict.

## Continuity artefacts

Repository or file tree, data or schema, README, test log, design and UX evidence, version conventions, individual contribution record, runnable handoff state.

## Runtime checks

Accounts, devices and connectivity; starter code and expected output; privacy and safe practice; debugging checkpoint; authorship and GenAI conditions. The [Digital Technologies Hub](https://www.digitaltechnologieshub.edu.au/) is useful for ideas, but remap everything to NSW outcomes (see `../resources-external.md`).
