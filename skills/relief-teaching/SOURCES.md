# Sources

Checked 2026-11-12.

- Structure (duty of care before lesson content, a named colleague rather than "the office", medical alerts with action and trained person, early-finisher and work-collection fields, the pack supplementing induction) adapted from easedup's `casual-relief-plan` skill (<https://github.com/easedup/skills>), rewritten for NSW secondary and extended to the casual teacher's side. No text copied.
- NSW Department of Education, PD-2013-0454, Work health and safety: casual and relief staff are workers owed the same WHS duties. <https://education.nsw.gov.au/policy-library/policies/pd-2013-0454>
- NSW Department of Education, Writing report comments (updated 2026-09-29): NSWEduChat is the endorsed AI tool; other free tools are not endorsed. Used here for the anonymisation rule. <https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/reporting-to-parents/report-comments>
- NESA Teacher Accreditation Manual A3 (current as of 2026-05-14): casual and part-time timeframes and the 160-day threshold, used for the day log. <https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/teacher-accreditation/resources/nsw-ta-manual/a3>
- The non-specialist reliever, paper fallback and no-practical rules are design decisions from practice, not policy citations.
