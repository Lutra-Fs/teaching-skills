# Technology 7–8

Mandatory Years 7–8 course. Course facts below are an 11 August 2026 routing snapshot: recheck the live course page, implementation advice and record of changes for each new cohort or version (see `../curriculum-lock.md`).

## Which syllabus

- [Technology 7–8 (2023)](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/overview/course) starts implementation in 2026. Technology Mandatory 7–8 (2017) stays available until December 2027.
- If the school still uses the 2017 syllabus, read that exact structure; do not import the 2023 focus-area names. Do not assume the old "four projects, four folios, 50-hour Digital Technologies" pattern applies to the 2023 syllabus.

## 2023 requirements

- All four focus areas across Years 7–8 (not in every unit): [Digital and communication technologies](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/fa8e5fb98c), [Engineering technologies and systems](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/faf90a0673), [Food and agricultural practices](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/faa6e5f8d5), [Materials and production processes](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/fadc5a8620).
- The mandatory 200-hour allocation under the current [ACE curriculum requirements](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/ace-rules/ace5/curriculum-requirements).
- Practical and project work for most course time; projects together cover the required content; document, communicate and evaluate design and production processes. A project may draw on one or several focus areas only if the course-level coverage map stays sound.

## Distinct courses and focus areas

- Digital and communication technologies is not the Computing Technology elective.
- Food and agricultural practices is not the Food Technology elective.
- Engineering technologies and systems is not the Engineering elective.
- Timber is a material or project context, usually inside Materials and production processes; it is not a course or a fifth focus area.

## Continuity artefacts

Design brief, research and ideation, drawings or algorithms, material or ingredient plan, prototype or product, process record, testing data, evaluation, safe-use competence, the next project's prerequisite knowledge.

Runtime checks for workshop, food or software lessons are in `tas-common.md`.
