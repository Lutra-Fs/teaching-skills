## Curriculum route

Start at [NESA Mathematics syllabuses](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/mathematics). Resolve exact course, cohort and implementation term.

Lock the Stage 6 syllabus version for each of Year 11 and Year 12 separately: during transition, different cohorts can use different versions.

For Mathematics K–10 (2022):

- preserve the Stage 4/5 Core–Paths structure;
- treat a Path as a flexible route through the content, revisited from evidence;
- embed Working mathematically with the selected content outcome;
- use formative evidence to determine progression, consolidation and stretch;
- keep Life Skills as a confirmed separate pathway.

For Stage 6, resolve the exact course: Mathematics Standard Year 11 or, where applicable, Mathematics Standard 1 versus Standard 2 in Year 12; Advanced; Extension 1; Extension 2; Numeracy; or Life Skills. Then lock Year 11/12, syllabus version, assessment/reporting requirements, HSC examination/course specifications and the school schedule. Course content comes from the resolved course, since `Mathematics Standard` alone names several.

## Mathematics lesson design

Specify:

1. The mathematical concept, skill and reasoning demand—not only a topic label.
2. Prerequisite concepts and the progression connection.
3. Intended evidence of understanding, fluency, reasoning, problem solving and communication as relevant.
4. Representations and connections among concrete, visual, symbolic, graphical, numerical and contextual forms.
5. Examples, non-examples, worked examples and deliberate variation.
6. A question sequence from prerequisite check through misconception probe to transfer.
7. Likely error patterns and what each error suggests.
8. Calculator, manipulatives, software or graphing-tool purpose.
9. How students show method/reasoning, not only final answers.

Use modelling and guidance fading when appropriate:

`worked example -> completion/guided problem -> independent problem -> varied/transfer problem`

Treat this as one possible hard-scaffold progression. Fade from error patterns, explanations and matched independent or transfer performance. Reintroduce only the missing prompt, representation or step where possible. When problem formulation, representation choice, strategy selection or justification is the target thinking, it stays with students.

## Differentiation

Use `../scaffolding-differentiation.md` for the common support protocol. In Mathematics, vary worked-example completeness, number complexity, representation and strategic hints from the identified barrier. Extend through structure, proof, generalisation, error analysis or application rather than extra repetitive questions.

## Mathematics evidence checks

Prefer checks that expose thinking:

- mini-whiteboard response plus method;
- compare two solutions;
- locate and repair an error;
- choose a representation and justify it;
- hinge question with diagnostically distinct distractors;
- short independent problem and explanation;
- exit response linked to the next lesson decision.

Define the branch before teaching: what answer pattern triggers reteaching, regrouping, another representation or stretch?

## Stage 4–5 emphasis

Use learner evidence to choose chunk size, scaffolding and Core/Path content. Prioritise foundations, mathematical habits, multiple entry points and growing independence without lowering the intended mathematics. Still monitor mandatory content and unit coherence.

## Stage 6 emphasis

Maintain a live tracker:

- required versus taught versus evidenced outcomes/content;
- lessons remaining and known disruptions;
- school assessment dates and HSC milestones;
- prerequisite gaps and recovery time;
- retrieval, interleaving, exam-style response and feedback cycles;
- consolidation/revision buffer.

Coverage counts as learned when students show it. Balance pacing with checks, depth, feedback and targeted recovery.

## Lesson template fields

Use the lesson template the school or university requires, filling its own fields; the teacher lists local template paths under `Local sources` in `_teacher_profile.md`. Content for those fields:

- unit position/lesson number;
- syllabus/cohort source reference;
- explicit concepts/skills and misconceptions;
- QTM evidence notes outside the live run sheet;
- outcome measurement/recording;
- WHS, references and attached resources.

APST descriptors in a worked example are targets.
