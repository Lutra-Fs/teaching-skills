# Teacher profile

Where this teacher's practice differs from the plan-teaching defaults. When the teacher confirms a new preference, add it here, dated.

## Planning

- Individual work by default; pairs or groups when the program wording or the class's `_course_context.md` asks for them. Grouping adds classroom-management load, so ask when the term stage may change it. (2026-10-30)
- Slides are a slide deck with on-screen text and speaker notes per slide; make a .pptx only when the teacher asks for that file. (2026-10)
- Lesson plans hold the APST Graduate targets as numbers (2.1, 3.2) and one or two sentences on what lies outside the syllabus and the cut order. (2026-11-05)

## Student text

- Citations use APA 7th. (2026-10-23)
- Google Classroom holds the Do Now and the assignment; everything else is discussed aloud. (2026-10-23)
- Work that branches off the main task gets its own "Think further:" heading. (2026-11-23)
