# Lesson 7: Encryption (draft)

Year 11 Software Engineering, Secure Software Architecture. Single period.

**Learning intention:** Students understand encryption.

## Sequence

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–15 | Recap: students finish their Lesson 6 OAuth flow diagrams and share two with the class. |
| 15–30 | Teacher explains symmetric encryption, asymmetric encryption, hashing, salting and the TLS handshake. |
| 30–55 | Pairs complete the Caesar cipher handout, then join another pair to form a group of four and prepare a short presentation on where encryption is used. |
| 55–70 | Individual research: compare AES and RSA online and write a paragraph. |
| 70–75 | Pack up. |

## Student handout

1. Decrypt this message with a shift of 3: KHOOR. (Answer: HELLO)
2. Encrypt your first name with a shift of 5.
3. Which headings apply to encryption? (Answer: confidentiality, integrity)
4. Explain why websites hash passwords instead of encrypting them.
   Model answer: Hashing is one-way, so even if the database is stolen the attacker cannot reverse the stored values into passwords.
