# Course context: Software Engineering, 2026-27 cohort

School facts for planning this class.

## Class

- Course: Year 12 Software Engineering (Stage 6), Secure Software Architecture module, taught to the Year 11 class (2026/27 cohort) by school decision.
- Class teacher and mentor: Ms Reid.
- Seven lessons per fortnight.

## Timetable

- No double periods. Every lesson is a single 60-minute period; design about 55 minutes of content (the first 5 minutes are arrival and the Do Now) and end with 2 minutes of pack-up.
- If content does not fit one period, split it into consecutive single lessons (as with Lesson 4: 4a evidence, 4b writing).

## Mentor direction (Ms Reid, 2026-10-19)

- Content delivery first. A brief recap or recall of unfinished work is fine, but never stay on the previous lesson.
- Keep feedback on submissions concise.

## Delivery
